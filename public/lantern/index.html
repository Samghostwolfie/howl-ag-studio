Lantern Ring

  • 💍 The ring goes on your right middle finger
  • ✊ Make a fist to power it up, then say “cube”, “ball”, “shield”, “sword”, “hammer” or “wall”
  • 🏮 Point at a table or the floor, then pinch the Lantern button with your hand
  • ⚡ Hold the ring to the lantern and speak the oath to charge it to 100%
  • 🔋 Knock the ring on a surface, or say “power”, to hear how much charge is left

Uses your camera and microphone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded except speech, which your browser sends to its speech service to understand commands. Fan-made tribute. Not affiliated with DC.
